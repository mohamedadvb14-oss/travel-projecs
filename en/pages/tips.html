Tips for a better trip

Small tips to help you enjoy travelling with comfort and respect for the place.

  1. Pick the right season

    Weather varies by place; check it before you go, especially for desert and mountain trips.

  2. Drink water regularly

    Carry water and stay hydrated on hot days and at open-air sites.

  3. Wear comfortable shoes

    Archaeological sites and old towns involve a lot of walking.

  4. Respect people’s privacy

    Ask before photographing people and follow photo rules in museums and sites.

  5. Agree on service details

    Ask about price and duration first, and keep your booking confirmations.

  6. Keep your belongings safe

    Keep documents and money safe and store digital copies of your travel papers.

  7. Protect heritage and nature

    Never write on monuments or touch exhibits, and take your waste with you.

  8. Plan your time wisely

    Do not overfill your day; leave time for rest, crowds and transfers.
